Tool

GRC Control Mapper

Reads a security policy and maps each requirement to NIST SP 800-53 controls, including the supplier controls SR-2 and SA-9. Each match shows why it scored the way it did, and weak matches go to an analyst review queue instead of being trusted blindly.

64 automated tests.

How I work

  1. 1

    Talk it through and map it

    Start with the people who know the work, then draw who depends on whom.

  2. 2

    Break it into connected concepts

    Split the problem into small ideas and show how each one drives the next.

  3. 3

    Automate the repeatable parts

    Turn the steps that repeat into tools or AI agent workflows, with a person checking the result.
